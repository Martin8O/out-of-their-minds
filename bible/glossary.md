# Glossary — «Не в своём уме» · Russian termbase (Rosman / Росмэн lexicon)

> **Lexicon law: Rosman (Росмэн) for every Harry Potter canon term and name — NEVER Spivak /
> Махаон variants.** One canonical Russian form per term, chosen once, held everywhere. Where a
> firm Rosman form exists it wins; where none exists, the chosen form below is the standard.
> Parent source (fuller tables, orthography rationale): `../../Translation/RU/glossary.md`.
> The gate lints the forbidden variants (style-guide §8).

## 1. Title

| Item | Russian (chosen) | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Novel title | **«Не в своём уме»** | locked in P1 (ADR-005): the idiom "out of one's mind" read literally — not in one's own mind/body. The body swap, the romcom madness, and falling in love against your own better judgment in three words; comedic on the shelf, thematically exact underneath (the book is about finally coming home to yourself). Runners-up logged: «Обмен любезностями», «Один очаг». |

## 6. Orthography & register pointers (full spec in `style-guide.md`)

- **магл lowercase**; capitals only on true proper nouns (Министерство магии, Хогвартс, Битва за
  Хогвартс, Чёрная Метка, Путаница, «Обряд одного очага» as a titled rite; the rite lowercase in
  running prose: *обряд*).
- **Dialogue is em-dash, not quotation marks**: *— Грейнджер, это моя рука.* «Ёлочки» reserved for
  titles, quoted documents, and written matter (letters, Prophet headlines, the manuscript's lines).
- **Hold one Russian form per term** — Russian synonymy makes drift the default failure; this file
  is the single source, and the gate enforces the forbidden-variant list.
- **Do not over-diminutivise names**: Тилли stays Тилли (not Тиллечка) except in deliberate
  intimacy beats; Гермиона never Герми except as a (rationed) Draco tease.
