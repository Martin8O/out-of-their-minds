# world-rules — «Не в своём уме»

> The invented magic of this book. Once written here, it is law: prose may not cheat it, and any
> extension is logged here + `invented-canon.md` in the same prompt. Canon magic per books 1–7.

## 1. «Обряд одного очага» (the Rite of One Hearth)

- **Provenance:** a rite of the First Hearths, millennia before wand civilisation, preserved in
  ONE ogham-scripted Old Brittonic manuscript (bronze-cornered; excavated in Wiltshire; catalogued
  by bureau «Наследие» as **лот 44 — «сундук из раскопок в Уилтшире»**, donated uninventoried to
  the orphanage charity auction — plant ch2).
- **Historical purpose:** ending blood feuds. The heads of two warring hearths would "unite" —
  live each other's lives until neither could remember whose grievance was whose. It was
  **communal**: held by an entire village circle standing witness.
- **The mistranslated verb:** the ancient verb the children render as «соединить» (to unite)
  originally meant **«прожить чужую жизнь»** — to experience another person's life. Spoken
  language drifted over centuries; the magic's literal meaning did not.
- **The wrong dictionary (fair-play, blameless):** Tilly translated *correctly* — from **Криспин
  Бэгшот's 1834 «Словарь старых наречий»**, a notoriously "romantic" era of glossing, which
  back-projects the modern sense «соединить навеки». Nobody was a fool. (Батильда Бэгшот's
  embarrassing relative — the footnote about the «нерадивый родственник» sits in a book on
  Hermione's own shelf, plant ch12.) The ancient root survives in the modern wizard **wedding
  word «кертан»** — audible to the reader at Ron's wedding (ch17) one part before the reveal.
- **Casting (2/3 May 2009, the attic):** two casters each hold "a thing warmed by the
  hearth-keeper" (Oscar: Hermione's handkerchief; Tilly: Draco's stolen silver button). The rite
  binds along **the newest hearth-vows touching the casters** — the guardianship papers signed at
  the gala hours earlier. It targets the guardians, not the children.

### Escalation ladder (the leads)
1. Night one–two: **shared dreams** — each dreams the other's memories (played as "strange
   dreams", ch3).
2. Day two: **leaking habits and skills** — her hand writes his ledger-script; he alphabetises
   her spice rack in his sleep.
3. Morning of day three (5 May): **full body swap**. Souls travel; bodies stay. Continuous until
   the unwind (ch29–31). POV convention: `pov:` = whose *soul* we ride.

### Why under-18s are immune (hard rule)
The rite's own written law, translated early and passed over as decoration (ch12):
**«дитя принадлежит всем очагам»** — a child carries no feud and is party to no quarrel; in the
rite's grammar children are «ещё не названные» — selves still being written, nothing completed
to exchange. The magic cannot count them as *sides*. (This is also the clue that the rite
predates every modern institution — it counts hearths, not wands, ch18.)

### Why Britain unravels (the anomalies' single law)
Performed by two children, mistranslated and **unanchored** — without the village circle the
rite was built to be held by — it drafts all of magical Britain as the missing circle. The
ambient magic starts **confusing identity, perspective, truth and personal experience** in
everyone of age, in waves. One law, every anomaly: *the magic is trying to make strangers
experience one another until the feud it cannot find burns out.*

- **Rhythm — HARD, and the single easiest thing in this book to get wrong.** «девять ночей —
  один круг» is the RITE's own count (ch12), not the waves' timetable. The **wave-to-wave
  intervals form no series at all**: 15 May → 29 May → 3 June → 13 June → 25 June = 14 / 5 / 10 /
  12. What shortens is the **LAG** between «первые одиночные случаи» and «общее начало» —
  11-8-4-2 → 0, and then 0-0-0 until the ninth line re-opens it to 2 (ch15 charts the
  falling series; plant P11, printed in the Prophet's own timelines, fair-play chartable).
  **The last warning returns and nobody reads it** — ch29: the rite gives two days'
  notice before the ninth wave and the country, having learnt that the lag is zero,
  has stopped looking for one. **No narrator and no character may state a nine-night wave rhythm as
  fact.** A lead may lay the rite's nine nights over the country as a working guess — and must
  be shown knowing the past intervals do not fit (Draco does exactly this in ch18; that is why
  Wave 6 on 4 July is a projection, not a law).
- **Deadline:** after the ninth circle closes (night of **9/10 August 2009**), every exchange
  «приживается» — takes root, permanent. The waves give nothing to extrapolate from, so the date
  stays unknown until **ch27**, where it comes out of the rite's own count and not the waves'
  (Theo's arithmetic).

### The reversal condition (hard rule; no counter-spell exists)
The rite **cannot be broken — only completed**. The clause **«пока один очаг не догорит»** is
first misread as "until a hearth burns" (they ceremonially burn a copy, ch14 — nothing, except
the funniest failed magic in the book; Tilly's margin correction: «не „сгорит“ — „догорит“»).
True reading (14th-century commentary in the Malfoy library — an ancestor once ended a feud and
a scandal by this very rite, ch26): the exchange ends when the two hearths have burned down
**into one** — when neither party guards a door against the other and both, of free will, choose
to remain at one hearth.

- **Second verb-trap (fair-play rhyme):** the completion clause they first read as «признаться»
  (confess) actually means **«вверить»** (entrust). A staged, checklist confession attempt fails
  (ch27) — *entrusting done as a tactic is not entrusting*. The rite checks nothing that can be
  performed.
- **GUARDRAIL (binding, the book's spine):** **the magic only RATIFIES what the humans have
  already done.** By ch29 the swap has begun dissolving on its own — the first clean, unswapped
  Patronus (her otter, in her true voice) arrives *before* anyone understands why — because the
  secrets were given and accepted for human reasons. The emotional arc causes the reversal,
  never the other way round. Magic never solves an emotional problem anywhere in this book.
- **Unwind:** across Britain in reverse order, **last-in-first-out** (reader-chartable elegance,
  ch31–33); custody paperwork revalidates as handwriting returns.

### Wave lifecycle (law; fixed for ch19+ after the Part II review)

1. **Three stages, always.** «Первые одиночные случаи» (taken by the country for its own
   clumsiness) → **общее начало**, the day it takes everyone at once → **осадок**: the effect
   drops to a weaker, rarer, more private background. Only the loud stage ends. A wave that has
   «отшумела» is still running underneath (ch17's line about the fourth wave says exactly this).
2. **Acute phase: 3–9 nights, never uniform.** W4 (13 June) was loud four or five days;
   W5 (25 June) is still loud on day six (30 June). Nobody in Britain can predict the length
   either — that is the point.
3. **Nothing ever switches off. Waves accumulate and overlap.** By the end of June the country
   lives under all five at once, differing only in loudness: transgression still spoils
   appearance, Patronuses still misdeliver, Floo still misroutes on a bad day. Any scene may use
   any earlier wave at background strength without announcement.
4. **A settled effect may flare and mutate — that is not a new wave.** W1's origami arrivals on
   25 June are W1 six weeks on, escalated. Flares have no «первые одиночные случаи» and no
   «общее начало» and are never counted in the numbered nine.
5. **W5's carve-out is stated law, not a gloss: the wave takes FEAR, not shame.** It fires on
   one whole sentence, aloud, only before the person the speaker fears being honest with, within
   ~five paces. Guilt, embarrassment and shame do not trigger it at any distance (ch18, Nott).
   Consequence, and it is load-bearing: when the wave goes silent between two people who were
   firing at each other a week ago, the fear is gone. That is a **diagnostic, never an
   exemption** — the rule is not permitted to bend for the leads' convenience anywhere.
6. **W8's carve-out is stated law, not a gloss: the wave takes the DECISION, not the deed, and
   it delivers the feeling UNLABELLED** (ch24). The eighth wave moves a feeling along an
   *intended action*: at the instant a person of age **decides** to do something to a specific
   other person — arrest them, refuse them, overtake them in a queue, reach across a table for
   their hand — they receive what that person is feeling at that moment, at full strength, **as
   their own**. Distance is irrelevant (this is the deliberate contrast with W5's five paces);
   what selects the target is the decision. Consequences, all binding:
   - **A decision never acted on still fires.** You can be flooded on account of a cruelty you
     only considered — and there is no outward sign, because deciding is invisible.
   - **The feeling arrives with no author attached.** It is indistinguishable from one's own by
     strength, texture or behaviour, and there is **no test, spell or procedure anywhere in this
     book that establishes whose a given feeling is**. This is not a puzzle awaiting a solution;
     it is the wave's law, and it must never be solved — the leads' whole predicament in ch24–ch26
     rests on it. Traffic is **two-way and simultaneous** whenever both parties decide.
   - **It makes feeling louder, never truer** — the §3 rail (magic never solves an emotional
     problem) is enforced here: nobody learns anything true about anybody from this wave.
   - **Public face:** arrests cannot be completed anywhere in Britain from 20 July; the DMLE's
     workaround is to split the deciding from the executing (ch24).
7. Children are immune to every wave, every stage, always — and under W8 immunity is proved to
   run **in both directions** (ch24, derived on-page from «дитя принадлежит всем очагам»,
   §1): a child neither receives an adult's feeling nor surrenders their own. **A decision aimed
   at a child returns nothing.** Load-bearing and fair-play: it is why the four adults deciding
   about them on 23 July cannot be made to feel what Tilly and Oscar feel, and why the one weapon
   that would settle it does not exist — the only two people at that table who cannot be read are
   the two being decided about. Do not let any later chapter quietly reopen this.

Rails: waves start funny, turn genuinely threatening from wave 6; the children are always
immune; national canvas stays garnish — every wave lands ON the household of four. New anomalies
beyond these two inventions: none without a logged extension here.

## 3. Hard rails (from the brief; binding)

- No traditional villain; the antagonist is two false beliefs (see `characters.md`).
- Magic may solve practical problems, never emotional ones (the guardrail above is structural).
- Physical intimacy only in their own bodies; the swap builds intimacy through *lives*, not
  bodies; each conspicuously refuses to snoop (the unread drawer, the unread letter).
- No resurrection, no time travel, no Voldemort revival; era-tech is 2009-plausible.
- The ending is constructive: the Argentine tango, late August 2009, at the orphanage ball.
