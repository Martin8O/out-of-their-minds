# plot-architecture — «Не в своём уме»

> The book as four parts → sequences → 33 chapters (cards: `chapter-grid.md`). Theme: *любовь
> возможна не благодаря совершенству, а благодаря уязвимости* — never spoken aloud in prose.

## 0. The three comedy machines (design law)

1. **DOUBLE LIFE** — each lead performs the other's job, friends, family; every scene carries the
   gap between who is speaking and who is seen; every laugh is an act of learning the other from
   the inside.
2. **ANOMALY** — one fresh public set piece per part; always funny for Britain, dangerous for the
   leads (the no-lying wave is canteen slapstick AND a guillotine over the pension).
3. **OBSERVERS** — Narcissa (tells), Harry (the list), the children, Живоглот: almost-catching
   them, so tension and comedy are one substance. (Rita = off-page pressure only.)

**Card-level law:** the funniest beat of every chapter must reveal something emotionally true;
no joke survives that its character wouldn't organically produce.

## 1. Part structure (each part escalates ONE question)

### Part I «Чужая мантия» — ch 1–9 (2–29 May)
**Question: can they survive each other's lives without being exposed?**
Gala and auction; the rite cast in the attic interlude; dreams → swap; ground rules; the double
set piece (Wednesday Tea / Burrow Sunday); waves 1–2; the sheep shed.
**Gate out:** hostility → grudging teamwork; the first honest «мы» (ch9); the manuscript trail
opens; «Пророк» names the crisis «Путаница».

### Part II «Один очаг» — ch 10–18 (June)
**Question: what is this rite and what does it want?**
Research comedy: the manuscript under Tilly's floorboard; the guilt bomb (lot 44 came through
his firm); Theo's translations with the anonymous margin-corrector; the Task-Force farce; the
burn-the-copy failure; the nine-night rhythm charted; Ron's wedding (the word «кертан» rings);
the Malfoy library gives the mistranslation.
**Gate out:** respect → friendship and unfamiliar safety; each has defended the other in public
unasked; the model flips — the rite is working *perfectly*; it was asked wrong.

### Part III «Девятый круг» — ch 19–26 (July)
**Question: can they keep the children — and each other's secrets — while Britain unravels?**
The hinge: waves turn threatening (no lying, dead signatures, owls reading letters, empathy
floods); the children's confession («Я неправильно перевела»); the «ты» shift; the custody
hearing and Oscar's three galleons; Narcissa's testimony and the 14th-century commentary — the
finish line is intimacy, not counterspell.
**Gate out:** protectiveness → shared vulnerability; both know what the cure requires and both
are terrified for the same reason: it demands being fully known.

### Part IV «Своими ногами» — ch 27–33 (August)
**Question: will they choose each other with nothing left hidden?**
The failed staged completion (вверить ≠ признаться); Manchester wakes swapped; her affair
entrusted; his pension entrusted; the first clean Patronus; Ron's grace, Harry's file; the
completion as a domestic act; Britain wakes; custody; Mrs Crabbe's letter; the ball and the
tango.
**Gate out (the book's last realization):** the children's mistranslated ritual worked exactly
as the children intended — one family, no one left behind — just not by magic.

## 2. Mystery spine (reader discovers WITH the leads; every reveal opens a question)

DISCOVERY (1–5): gala; auction; attic interlude (no explanation); the waking; ground rules.
LINKAGE (6–10): Путаница hits the press; epicentre mapped to the night of 2 May; Draco suspects
a cursed auction artifact; audit finds ONE uncatalogued item.
RESEARCH (11–15): manuscript found (child-pencil margins, unattributed); Theo's first
translation — blessing lines read as decoration; Task Force; burn-the-copy; the rhythm charted.
MISTRANSLATION REVEAL (16–18): thoughts-aloud wave; the wedding word; 1834 vs the older
glossary: «прожить чужую жизнь». The rite is flawless; the asking was wrong.
CONFESSION (19–21): the no-lying wave corners the children; «в этой семье правду говорят
бесплатно».
RACE + REVERSAL REVEAL (22–26): signatures die; hearing; Narcissa's commentary — completed, not
broken.
THE RACE (27–31): failed staging; the deadline arithmetic; two entrustings; the ratifying magic;
completion at one hearth.
RESOLUTION (32–33): LIFO unwind; custody; the letter that always knew; the tango.

## 4. Set pieces (minimum four full comic set pieces — gridded)

1. **Wednesday Tea / Burrow Sunday double-header** (ch5+ch8) — first performances of each other.
2. **Task Force** (ch13) — Hermione's post hunts their own secret with Draco's soul in the chair;
   the voices wave detonates mid-session.
3. **Open Day at «Крапивный дом»** (ch16) — origami-трансгрессия, the bake-sale scandal, the
   thoughts-aloud wave among prospective parents.
4. **Diagon Alley owl apocalypse** (ch22) — privacy dies in public; the planner page.
5. *(pivot)* **The custody hearing** (ch25) — where the comedy turns into stakes.

## 5. Intimacy architecture (18+, per brief; binding)

- Balance across the book: **60–70 %** interiority/longing · **20–35 %** tension, proximity,
  touches, kisses · **~10 %** explicit — TWO full scenes: ch31–32 region (first night in their
  own bodies — built on a funny-tender misunderstanding: each knows the other's body from the
  *inside*, neither knows it from here) and the tango night (ch33, curtain drawn late).
- Physical intimacy only in their own bodies — the swap builds intimacy through lives.
- First kiss: ch30 (completion eve, own faces returning); the near-kiss farce ch24 (kissing your
  own face problem) is the comic rehearsal.
- Every intimate beat permanently changes trust/openness/self-acceptance — no fan-service.
