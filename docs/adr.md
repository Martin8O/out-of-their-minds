| ADR-008 | 2026-09-04 | **The book gets its own local git repository at the project directory** (ADR-006's «later» is now), initialised at P2 close with `Local/` ignored. **No remote, nothing pushed.** The parent Unplottable repo keeps ignoring the project directory. | A finished 103k-word manuscript, its bible and its ledger were living unversioned on one machine, which is the single largest avoidable risk in the project. | ADR-009 | 2026-09-05 | **The book ships with ONE fully realized intimate scene (head of ch32) and a curtain that falls with the needle in ch33** — not the two scenes `plot-architecture.md` §5 carded. Measured explicit prose ≈ 960 words ≈ 0.9 % of the book, ≈ 4 % of romance-bearing material, against the brief's ~10 %. The ch32 scene was deepened in P3 (the euphemistic summary «тепло, тесно и неловко» cut, concrete escalation added in the scene's own register); the second was not written. `plot-architecture.md` §5 amended so the bible no longer promises what the book does not deliver. | The P3 review panel (L5-01) was right that promise and delivery had come apart, and right that this is a Tier-0 shortfall against the commissioning brief. But the two candidate remedies both cost more than they pay: ch33's hard word band leaves ~380 words, and the only place a second scene fits is *after* «Игла сошла к середине и пошла по пустому кругу… и снять её никто не встал» — displacing the book's best last line for a compliance percentage. The brief's own rule («physical intimacy grows only out of emotional intimacy»; «the reader should feel every intimate scene could only happen at that exact point») is better served by one earned scene and a late curtain than by a second scene written to a quota. Logged, not hidden: if the commissioner wants the tango night on the page, it is a scoped P4 of ~600 words plus a re-cut ending. | accepted |
| ADR-010 | 2026-09-05 | **P3 triaged rather than obeyed its review panels**: 117 of 156 findings applied, 12 rejected with reasons, the rest deferred to a named residual list (`Local/review/TRIAGE.md`). Four confident findings — including one graded *blocker* — were verified as misreadings of deliberate work and rejected. Chapter-level findings (ch23's off-page deposition, the running jokes that stop running, four "perform the other's life" chapters in Part I) were deferred rather than patched, per the escalation rule. | Panels are lenses, not authorities: the standard (`Local/Prompts requirements.md` §3) requires evidence over vibes in both directions, and a finding that survives verification is worth ten that do not. Patching a chapter-level weakness in place is exactly what the escalation rule forbids; deferring it with a named fix keeps the option open at full cost visibility. | accepted |
| ADR-011 | 2026-09-05 | **The A5 PDF sets to 378 pages, not the brief's "180–200 printed pages"**, and the word count wins. Body 10.5pt Palatino Linotype, A5 with 1.8cm margins, typeset by tectonic. | The brief's two size specs contradict each other: 90–110k words cannot occupy 180–200 A5 pages, which would require ~540 words a page against the ~290 this geometry actually sets. Reaching 190 pages would mean either cutting the book by two thirds or type nobody can read. The word count and the 30–35 chapter count are the specs the whole project was built to (grid target sum 102 200), so the page estimate is the one that yields. If a slimmer object is ever wanted the lever is trim size, not type size — 6×9in would land near 250 pages. | accepted |
| ADR-012 | 2026-09-05 | **ch32 split into two chapters — 34 in total.** «Опись не сходится» (ch32, 14 Aug, the private night and the morning after) and «Обратный порядок» (ch33, 15 Aug, the unwind, the corridor, the verdict, the letter); «Веди» becomes ch34. ~1 750 words of new prose: a second, freer movement before dawn on her terms and without a list, the lamp she set as her one condition and puts out herself, the failed pricing where his oldest belief breaks on an event; and on the public side, the ministry corridor where her recusal is finally charged in front of him, a face for the crewman his widows' provision costs him, and three of the eleven widows named. Explicit prose roughly doubles, to ~1 900 words. | Three independent findings converged on the same chapter: L3-05 (ch32 carried a week's unwind, a bank, a bureau, a verdict, a letter, an invitation *and* the book's one explicit scene), L5-02 (that scene declined its physical clause) and L5-06 (Hermione chooses the recusal in ch29 and the price is thereafter only ever administered off-page, so Draco's lie — that loving him would stain her — is retired by fiat while every other belief in the book dies on the page). Splitting fixes all three at once and gives each half a full chapter's room; both halves land inside the target band, which a bare split could not have done. The brief permits 30–35 chapters. **ADR-009 still stands**: this is one explicit scene made whole, not the second scene the bible had carded, and the ending keeps its curtain. | accepted |
