| ADR-009 | 2026-09-05 | **The book ships with ONE fully realized intimate scene (head of ch32) and a curtain that falls with the needle in ch33** — not the two scenes `plot-architecture.md` §5 carded. Measured explicit prose ≈ 960 words ≈ 0.9 % of the book, ≈ 4 % of romance-bearing material, against the brief's ~10 %. The ch32 scene was deepened in P3 (the euphemistic summary «тепло, тесно и неловко» cut, concrete escalation added in the scene's own register); the second was not written. `plot-architecture.md` §5 amended so the bible no longer promises what the book does not deliver. | The P3 review panel (L5-01) was right that promise and delivery had come apart, and right that this is a Tier-0 shortfall against the commissioning brief. The brief's own rule («physical intimacy grows only out of emotional intimacy»; «the reader should feel every intimate scene could only happen at that exact point») is better served by one earned scene and a late curtain than by a second scene written to a quota. Logged, not hidden: if the commissioner wants the tango night on the page, it is a scoped P4 of ~600 words plus a re-cut ending. | accepted |
| ADR-010 | 2026-09-05 | **P3 triaged rather than obeyed its review panels**: 117 of 156 findings applied, 12 rejected with reasons, the rest deferred to a named residual list (`Local/review/TRIAGE.md`). | ADR-011 | 2026-09-05 | **The A5 PDF sets to 378 pages, not the brief's "180–200 printed pages"**, and the word count wins. | ADR-013 | 2026-09-06 | **Draco's secret is replaced: eleven years of second Sundays, not money.** The forged pension, the counterfeit drafts, the leather case of evidence, the 1997 coinage and the Gringotts sack are gone.
